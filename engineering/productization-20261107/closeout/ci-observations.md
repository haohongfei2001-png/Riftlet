The maintained offline suite loads the archived VM program unchanged. Its eight scenarios wait for the corresponding observable events, with a finite timeout that fails on a missing event. The native job runs all non-arrival methods in parallel and the complete live `ArrivalModesTests` class once serially. It compares the actual methods in both transcripts with SwiftPM discovery; an empty partition, a missing or duplicate method, or a failed serial test fails the job. The new diagnostics record existing arrival events on an observation failure. No animation timing, capture qualification, upload rule, security gate or consumer preference changes.

The earlier source/admission and its successful jobs are historical records. Use the **current head's five checks** and the **actual main push checks** linked from [PR #47](https://github.com/haohongfei2001-png/Riftlet/pull/47) for the final integration; no result from the failed head substitutes for them.
