## Description

Picture a question about two tidal maps. Both show the same stretch of shoreline. Use your usual Mac screenshot method to save a picture of each map.

Watch the tray in this recording of Riftlet on a Mac. It brings each saved crop to the edge, where you can pick out the detail you want. To use it with ChatGPT, bring the editable draft to the front in Google Chrome and click the matching thumbnail. Check the attachment there, then finish writing the question.

A click chooses the picture. You decide when the message is ready. This film shows the desktop side of that workflow.

## FAQ

**What does the disappearing thumbnail tell me?**

ChatGPT has begun receiving that particular image. Check its attachment area to see how the upload is going.

**What if I only want to collect screenshots for now?**

Grant access to the screenshot folder. You can browse local history with the Chrome integration off. Screen Recording permission is needed for Riftlet's built-in capture tools.

**Where was the two-image attachment actually tested?**

On an installed build 18, two separate screenshots joined the same real ChatGPT draft with the earlier text and attachment intact. The test ended with the message unsent. Build 20 uses the same handoff logic.
