# Product Hunt

## Product name

Riftlet

## Tagline

Click a Mac screenshot to attach it to a ChatGPT draft

## Listing description

Take a screenshot on your Mac and a thumbnail appears at the edge of your screen. Bring your ChatGPT draft to the front in Chrome, then click the image you want to attach. Finish the thought and send it when you're ready.

## Maker's first comment

The interesting part of a screenshot is often one small detail. Say you're looking at two tidal maps and want to ask about a bend in the shoreline. You know which bit you mean. Finding the right timestamped file is a detour.

Riftlet gives each new Mac screenshot a place at the edge of your screen. Bring the editable ChatGPT draft you're writing in to the front in Google Chrome and choose the thumbnail by sight. As ChatGPT starts receiving that image, the thumbnail tucks away. You can still change the question before you send it.

You can download the Public Beta from the Riftlet website. It runs on macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. What do you usually want to point at when you add a screenshot to a question?

## Questions from the launch page

**Can I use my usual screenshot shortcut?**

Yes. Let macOS save the screenshot as a file and give Riftlet access to that folder. A clipboard-only capture skips the tray.

**Which draft does my click go to?**

Bring the editable ChatGPT draft you want to the front in Google Chrome. Riftlet targets that draft. The ChatGPT desktop app and other browsers are outside this Beta's scope.

**Where can I check the upload?**

Look in the draft's attachment area. The thumbnail retracts when ChatGPT starts receiving the image. You control the Send button.
