## Note

Try this next time you're reading two maps side by side. Crop the same patch of coast on each, then look at the little pictures near the edge of your Mac. One glance is enough to tell the two crops apart.

That little tray is Riftlet. It uses the screenshots your Mac already saves. Open the ChatGPT draft you want in Google Chrome and click the picture that goes with your question. You can still change the wording before you send it.

Unused thumbnails archive after two minutes by default. If one slips away while you're reading, open Screenshot History from the menu bar and restore the archived image to the desktop.

## Replies to save with the note

**My screenshot went to the clipboard. Will it show up?**

Use Shift–Command–4 and let macOS save a file. Adding Control copies to the clipboard only, which leaves the tray without a new file to collect.

**Can I keep a screenshot around while I write?**

Yes. In Settings, adjust the idle archive time to 1–10 minutes or turn it off. Archived images can be restored from Screenshot History.
