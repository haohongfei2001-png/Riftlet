# Press copy · 媒体文案

## English

**Film title**

Light study

**Standfirst**

Two lines of light cross on the table. One small crop shows exactly where.

**Short product description**

Riftlet keeps Mac screenshots at the edge of your screen. Bring an editable ChatGPT draft to the front in Google Chrome, click the picture you want, and continue writing.

**Release note**

Riftlet 0.3.5 (20) is available as a Mac Public Beta. The app collects screenshot files saved by macOS. Clicking a thumbnail attaches the selected image to the current editable ChatGPT draft in Google Chrome. When the page begins receiving the image, the thumbnail retracts and the next one can be selected. The user sends the message. Screenshot copies and history are stored on the Mac.

**Film credit**

Light study is a Riftlet brand film told through design animation. The optics study is original campaign artwork.

## 中文

**片名**

光的路径

**引言**

两道光，在桌面上相交。截下这一小处，问题就有了落点。

**产品简介**

Riftlet 把 Mac 截图留在屏幕边缘。将可编辑的 ChatGPT 草稿切到 Google Chrome 前台，点一下想用的微图，就可以接着写。

**公开版说明**

Riftlet 0.3.5（20）Mac 公开测试版现已提供下载。App 会收录 macOS 保存的截图文件。用户点击微图，即可将选中的图片附加到 Google Chrome 前台可编辑的 ChatGPT 草稿。页面开始接收时，这张微图会收起，可以继续选择下一张。消息由用户发送。截图副本和历史记录保存在 Mac 本机。

**影片署名**

《光的路径》为 Riftlet 品牌设计动画。片中光学玻璃图为此次推广制作的原创视觉素材。
