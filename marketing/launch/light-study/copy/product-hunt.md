# Product Hunt

## English listing

**Name**

Riftlet

**Tagline**

Click a Mac screenshot to attach it to your ChatGPT draft

**Description**

Take your usual Mac screenshot. Riftlet keeps a thumbnail at the edge of your screen. Bring your ChatGPT draft to the front in Google Chrome, click the picture you want, and keep writing. You decide when to send.

**Maker’s first comment**

Look at the two lines of light in the film. You could spend a whole sentence explaining which tiny crossing you mean. A crop does it in a second.

I made Riftlet for the moment after that crop. The screenshot waits at the edge of the Mac. When the right ChatGPT draft is in front in Chrome, one click puts the picture beside the question. Once the page starts receiving the image, there’s room to pick the next one.

You can try the public beta on macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. If your work involves asking questions about images, what detail did you last have to circle, crop or point out?

**Gallery caption**

Two views of a shoreline wait side by side in the native tray. Pick the crop that belongs with your question.

## 中文产品页

**名称**

Riftlet

**标语**

点一下，把 Mac 截图附到 ChatGPT 草稿里

**简介**

在 Mac 上照常截图，Riftlet 把微图留在屏幕边缘。将正在写的 ChatGPT 草稿切到 Google Chrome 前台，点一下想用的图，就能接着写。消息由你发送。

**开发者首评**

片中那块玻璃投下两道光。想问它们在哪里相交，光是描述位置就得写一整句。截下交点，让人一眼就能找到。

我做 Riftlet，想省的是截好图之后找文件的那一步。图先在 Mac 的屏幕边缘等着。等 Chrome 前台是想用的那份 ChatGPT 草稿，再点一下微图。页面开始接收这张图，就可以接着选下一张。

公开测试版支持 macOS 13 及以上的 Apple 芯片和 Intel Mac。上次你想指给别人看的，是图片里的哪个细节？

**图库配文**

同一段岸线的两张潮汐图并排待用。按图样，挑出和问题有关的那张。
