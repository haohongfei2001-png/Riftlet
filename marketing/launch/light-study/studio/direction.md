# Light study · 光的路径
A designer is looking at two crossing rays. The film follows the tiny piece of the photograph that answers their question. The intersection holds its identity as the surrounding scale changes from physical specimen, to screenshot, to thumbnail, to draft attachment. The only contrasting image is a second view of the same specimen. This gives the viewer a reason to choose one of two tiles.
