# Riftlet — Official reference research and bilingual narrative

Research date: 2026-10-30 (Asia/Shanghai). This document is a design input, not a release certification.

## Scope and evidence boundary

All ten requested official websites were opened and read directly, including their longer page content and linked product descriptions. Four more directly relevant alternatives were checked against first-party documentation, developer repositories, or their official store listings. This researcher did not control a browser or inspect live rendered animations; the main design task owns rendered-page review. Accordingly, this document makes no unverified claims about exact fonts, color values, motion timing, visual pixel quality, logo geometry, or observed app reliability. Competitor feature statements below are vendor descriptions, not independent performance tests. No third-party artwork or fonts were copied into the Riftlet assets.

## 1. The finding that changes the positioning

Riftlet must not imply that placing screenshots at an edge, moving them into other apps, attaching them to an existing AI conversation, or withholding automatic sending is unique by itself.

- Dropover explicitly offers docked shelves at the edge, watched folders, file actions and sharing. [S08]
- CleanShot explicitly presents capture-to-other-app drag and drop as a central workflow. [S06]
- AI Screenshot, an official Chrome Web Store listing updated 2026-10-24, describes attachment to selected existing ChatGPT or Claude conversations without automatically sending, as well as a continuous capture queue. This listing establishes competing intent and a distributed extension; it does not establish reliability, adoption, or parity with a native Mac app. [S11]
- A public macOS project, screenshot-to-ai, describes watching screenshot folders and pasting into selected Chrome AI tabs. Its README is evidence of a published implementation claim, not an independent quality assessment. [S12]
- ChatGPT's own Appshots documentation describes frontmost-window capture, a configurable hotkey, and a configurable Current chat destination. Therefore “screenshots enter your current conversation” is also a native-platform capability, not a new category claim. [S13]

**Strategic inference:** Riftlet's credible opportunity is the quality of the entire handoff: familiar screenshot habits → visible, restrained edge staging → deliberate selection → protected current draft → honest progress and recovery. The brand should make that small sequence unmistakable and desirable. It should not enlarge its claims to compensate for the smallness of the product.

## 2. Seven deep references

### mymind

Observed headline: “Remember everything. Organize nothing.” [S01]

The sentence links a desirable outcome to an avoided maintenance burden. The page then grounds its mental-space metaphor in recognizable content types and concrete saving/search tasks. Its manifesto enlarges the emotional reason to use the tool, while feature sections give operational meaning to that promise.

**Absorb:** establish one central relief, then repeatedly demonstrate it. **Avoid:** borrowing its mind/storage narrative, absolute promises, or anti-organization rhetoric. Riftlet moves selected visual context into a draft; it is not a second brain. The useful lesson is semantic economy, not a similar slogan or color palette.

### Claude

Observed headline: “Question what’s next”; the page describes a thinking partner. [S02]

Its language gives the user the active intellectual role and positions the product as assistance. The first visible action leads into actual use, and plan/FAQ material explains scope afterward.

**Absorb:** respectful language that makes the person the actor; an unambiguous primary action. **Avoid:** importing its identity, emblem, humanistic styling, or broad cognitive promise. Riftlet has no independent reasoning capability. “You choose what to show; you decide when to send” is more accurate than personifying it as an assistant.

### Craft

Observed headline: “Your space for notes, tasks, and big ideas.” [S03]

The page connects categories to lived examples: writing, planning and organizing appear alongside concrete documents and tasks. Customization supports the idea of a personal working space. The content structure demonstrates how a general promise becomes a user's own activity.

**Absorb:** show believable material and a finished task, with typography and content working together. **Avoid:** treating Riftlet as a document workspace or copying a card-rich universe that would conflict with its thin-edge interface. One selected chart, paragraph or interface detail is stronger than a decorative wall of screenshots.

### Linear

Its homepage identifies the product category and intended users immediately, then shows detailed product states and workflows. Intake, planning, automation, review and shipping form a coherent story. [S04]

**Absorb:** claims should be accompanied by specific product behavior; consistent hierarchy makes complexity readable. **Avoid:** adopting enterprise scale, dense technical vocabulary, simulated agents or a dark dashboard merely to appear advanced. Riftlet's main loop is small enough to understand by interacting with it once.

### Raycast

Observed headline: “Your shortcut to everything.” [S05]

The explanation locates its broad benefit in an extensible launcher. Subsequent sections organize extensions, keyboard actions and AI around that same access mechanism. The homepage has a clear conceptual center despite a large feature set.

**Absorb:** make the interaction mechanism the organizing brand idea. **Avoid:** copying breadth, power-user maximalism, keyboard spectacle or numerical reliability claims. Riftlet's analog is one edge and one deliberate handoff, with supporting features subordinate to it.

### CleanShot X

Its official site presents a Mac capture toolkit spanning screenshots, recordings, editing and collaboration. A prominent section explains immediate sharing from the captured item, followed by concrete editing and cloud workflows. [S06]

**Absorb:** communicate with visible before/after outcomes and real tasks; make distribution, support and platform scope easy to find. **Avoid:** competing by listing every screenshot feature. CleanShot already owns a broad, mature capture/edit/share story. Riftlet should explain the specific moment after capture rather than imply that all screenshot tools force users through folders.

### Dropover

The site starts from drag-and-drop friction and explains a temporary shelf, then extends into actions, sharing and customization. Edge docking and watched folders are explicitly listed. [S08]

**Absorb:** a spatial metaphor is convincing when it is also the actual interaction. **Avoid:** calling any edge shelf novel or replacing Riftlet's peeking, original-ratio thumbnails with a generic large tray. Riftlet's opportunity is the restrained edge treatment and intentional connection to the current supported draft; Dropover remains broader for moving arbitrary files.

## 5. Recommended message architecture

### Brand essence

**Riftlet makes visual context easy to bring into an ongoing conversation.**

Its category description should stay concrete: **a native Mac screenshot companion for supported ChatGPT drafts**. Its broader brand theme can be **a quiet passage from seeing to saying**. The latter is a creative interpretation, not a capability claim.

The edge is valuable because it is a stable place to retrieve what the user has just seen. The act of clicking resolves intention: this image, for this conversation, now. The disappearance is meaningful only after positive upload-start evidence; it must never become a theatrical claim of completion.

Supporting tools and recovery copy must be matched to the actual tested scope from the repository audit. In uncertain states, the site must not show an unconditional success or automatic retry.

### Demo microcopy and state semantics

1. Initial: **Take a screenshot / 截取一张**. Display original draft text already present.
2. Staged: **Ready at the edge / 已停在边缘**. Thumbnail button is keyboard reachable and named.
3. Clicked: **Adding to draft… / 正在附加…**. Do not remove the image immediately.
4. Positive simulated upload-start: **Upload started / 已开始上传**. This signal permits the fast inward disappearance.
5. Draft state: **Added to the draft. Not sent. / 已附加到草稿，未发送。** Only show this after the simulated attachment is present.
6. Confirmed failure: **Couldn’t attach. Your screenshot is back. / 未能附加，截图已恢复。** Retry must not duplicate an attachment.
7. Uncertain: **Check the draft before trying again / 请先查看草稿，再决定是否重试**. Keep the visual state distinct from confirmed failure.

Avoid showing internal enums, READY, adapter names, confidence scores or branch SHA in the product demo. Those belong in a developer review panel or handoff document.

### First public introduction — release-ready template

**English:** Meet Riftlet, a small Mac utility that keeps your latest screenshots at the edge of the screen. Choose one, and it is added to your current supported ChatGPT draft, alongside the words you have already written. Review it and send when you are ready. Riftlet is built around a simple moment: showing what you mean without losing your place in the conversation.

**中文：** 介绍 Riftlet：一个轻驻 Mac 屏幕边缘的小工具。刚截下的图片暂存在边缘，点选一张，就能附加到当前受支持的 ChatGPT 对话草稿，与你写好的文字放在一起。确认后，再由你发送。Riftlet 关心的是一个很小、却经常发生的瞬间：把眼前看到的内容带进对话，让思路继续。

This is a copy template. Until a public build and accurate compatibility statement exist, the actual candidate must visibly say **Design preview / 设计预览** and use the demo/development CTAs. Do not publish the template as an available-product announcement.

## 6. Principles to carry into the actual artwork

1. Let one boundary perform the same role in the logo, page composition and demo. Repetition of a mechanism produces identity more credibly than a collection of decorative motifs.
2. Let recognizable screenshot content supply warmth and visual variety. Keep the container calm.
3. Preserve a clear causal sequence. The screenshot does not disappear merely because the visitor clicks; the simulated upload-start signal must happen first.
4. Keep “selected attachment” and “sent message” visually separate. Never animate an assistant answer after attaching, because that would imply an unauthorized send.
5. Reserve strong color for the brand and decisive actions; use neutral surfaces for reading. Exact palette decisions require rendered comparison.
6. The desktop edge can be enlarged for explanation, but label explanatory magnification and never present a large gallery as the native interface.
7. Mobile should show an intentional portrait composition with a tactile simplified sequence, rather than a tiny unreadable desktop.
8. A sparse site can still feel unfinished. The work must come from proportion, meaningful movement, precise wording and complete states, not from deleting content indiscriminately.

## 8. Remaining research limits

This comparison does not establish trademark clearance, global logo uniqueness, accessibility compliance, measured conversion, comparative app reliability or exhaustive market coverage. Logo similarity needs a deliberate visual check against relevant marks and, before commercial registration, jurisdiction-specific clearance if pursued. Live rendered visuals and the new candidate's responsive/motion testing belong to the main design/QA record.
