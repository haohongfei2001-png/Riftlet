The project's own handoff commit `6f4156988f32fa06d4df9f06a43f564b560e0a1d` is imported unchanged under `research/web-attachment-handoff-2026-10-27/` (56 files, including the 55-entry manifest). `Sources/RiftletAdapters/Resources/chatgpt-web-20261027.js` explicitly attributes its derived File/DataTransfer/native-setter/change primitive to `src/input/shared-adapter.js`, its per-file/card observation to the two observer templates, and its exact-pixel/content rules to the preview-proof and attached classifiers. Changes include bounded chunk input, full Core operation/target binding, an asynchronous hash followed by a synchronous final input guard, bound final-preview proof, transient-node/source invalidation and observation-only recovery. The independently written integration is restricted to generated-image acceptance pending live integrated validation. Only this reviewed resource is bundled; archived Python drivers, raw diagnostic logs, images and `src/lab-only` fault/network hooks are excluded from the application. Existing third-party notices remain intact; no new third-party runtime dependency or license grant is introduced.

## Reuse decisions during parallel development

The implementation lead selects reuse without a routine user-approval step. Start with the pinned source above, inspect the exact required functions, compare their adaptation cost with a small native implementation, and record the result. Do not repeat broad competitive research or fork a complete upstream app to unblock one module. Retain the license/copyright/NOTICE in the same increment that introduces copied or adapted code; attribution is not a release-only follow-up. Current use of Sukurini/Screenshoss, independent Tendedero-informed panels and reference-only Oracle/Maccy must remain distinguishable in progress reports.
