# Your first screenshot

1. Open Riftlet from Applications. Quick Start shows the folder where your Mac saves screenshots. If access is requested, select that folder.
2. Choose **Try a Screenshot**. Use macOS Screenshot to capture a window or an area and save it as a file. Its thumbnail appears at the edge of the screen.
3. Bring the ChatGPT draft you want to use to the front in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Click the thumbnail. If the browser needs permission, follow the prompt for that browser. In Chrome, **Allow JavaScript from Apple Events** is under **View → Developer**. After setup, return to your draft and scroll up over the tray to choose the screenshot again.
4. Clicking the screenshot moves it into recent history. Check the attachment in your draft, add another image or send your message when ready.

**Use a screenshot again.** Scroll up over the tray to see recent screenshots. Click the one you want to attach. If the previous result was uncertain, check what is already in the draft first. Scroll down to return to the active tray. When the tray is empty, the clock button at the edge of the screen opens recent screenshots.

**Make the tray yours.** Open **Settings → Screenshot Tray** to change its position, appearance or idle timeout. Hiding desktop screenshots keeps local collection running.

**Reopen the guide.** Choose **Settings → Help → Quick Start**. To update an existing installation, choose **Check for Updates** from the Riftlet menu.
