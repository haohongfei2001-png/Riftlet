# Riftlet 1.0.10

点击截图发起附加后，它会从当前托盘移入最近截图。无论草稿接收图片、附加失败或结果暂时无法确认，都可以在历史中找回这张图。

在托盘上向上滑动查看最近截图，向下滑动返回当前托盘。需要再附加时，先查看当前草稿里已有的附件，再点击历史中的截图。Riftlet 仅在你选择图片时发起附加，消息仍由你发送。

Click a screenshot to attach it and move it from the active tray into recent history. It stays in history whether the draft accepts it, the attachment fails, or the outcome is uncertain.

Scroll up over the tray for recent screenshots, and scroll down to return to the active tray. To attach an image again, check the attachments already in your draft and click the image in history. Sending the message stays in your hands.
