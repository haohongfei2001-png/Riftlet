# Riftlet 1.0.11

如果点击截图时没有可用的 ChatGPT 草稿，后来这张图又被挤出最近 20 张，现在可以在完整截图仓库找到它，点击「恢复到托盘」。准备好草稿后，再点一次缩略图即可附加原图。

刚打开 ChatGPT 时，上传控件可能稍后才就绪。现在点选截图时，Riftlet 可以使用刚刚启用的原文件控件。

If you clicked a screenshot when no ChatGPT draft was available and it has since left your 20 recent screenshots, you can now find it in the full library and choose Restore to Tray. When your draft is ready, click the thumbnail to attach the original image.

On a newly opened ChatGPT draft, the upload field can take a moment to become available. Clicking a screenshot can now use that original field as soon as it is ready.
